## 🔊 Understanding Granular Synthesis

This software uses granular synthesis. It takes a small piece of sound and breaks it into tiny grains. You define the length of these grains. You can change how fast the grains play back. You can also change the pitch of each grain. 

The software creates dense clouds of sound when you play many grains at once. This creates texture. Complex textures appear when you change the playback direction of the grains. You can create rhythmic loops or drone sounds. Spend time with the knobs to learn how they affect the grains. Small changes lead to large shifts in the audio output.
